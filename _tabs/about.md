# About Cryptographic Audits

Cryptographic Audits publishes independent, version-specific technical notes on cryptographic research. Each article aims to make a claim testable: it identifies the exact manuscript version and location, states the disputed equation or interface, and provides an explicit counterexample or reproducible check where possible.

The goal is correction, not attribution of intent. Findings apply to the cited version of a paper as written; they do not automatically extend to later revisions or to every construction in the same family.

## Editorial principles

- **Version-specific:** every audit names the manuscript version examined.
- **Reproducible:** concrete field elements, equations, and minimal programs are preferred over impressionistic criticism.
- **Scoped:** correctness failures, security gaps, and missing specification are distinguished from one another.
- **Correctable:** substantive responses, fixes, and independent reproductions are welcome.

Source material and revision history are available through the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/cryptographic-audits/cryptographic-audits.github.io) and [cxcx52's public Gists](https://gist.github.com/cxcx52).
